# Research notebook workflow

This repository records the motivation, experiments, and contributions to
hodge-computer/sextics. Keep its fixed targets and existing upstream files intact.

For every substantive research batch, include a reproducible internal
visualization and link it from the result note and LEDGER.md. Prefer actual
operators, equations, parameter histories, and proof dependencies over generic
illustrations. State what the picture encodes and what it cannot establish.

Keep unsuccessful runs, search bounds, stop reasons, exact checks, attribution,
and corrections alongside successful results. Record experimental rationale
and evidence; pictures and a public log do not themselves prove correctness or
intent. Distinguish numerical observations, exact identities, complete family
exclusions, formal proofs, and accepted submissions.

Continue using Eisenbud's syzygy and graded-module viewpoint where it supplies
a concrete test. Do not claim a full resolution or geometric interpretation
from a truncated matrix calculation. Update the public-contribution tally only
after an actual upstream contribution, with its permanent link.
