# Sextics monitor, 13 November 2026

## Prescribed support and the universal target list

Chapter 1 now attributes algebraization **on the original principal open** `U = ℙⁿ ∖ V(D)` to Benjamin Diamond’s revised Theorem 2.13. The previous chapter pin described an extra restriction to the open where a chosen partial derivative of `F` is nonzero. In the revised account, Theorems 2.7 and 2.13 together give an algebraic primitive on `U` when the residue vanishes there on the hypersurface. The pole order along the supplied divisor is a separate step. Chapter 1 retains the bound `D_red^q` for a supplied support and gives no degree bound for finding one. It also now states Theorem 2.3 at each filtration level, as the sum of the residues of pole order at most `q+1` and the ambient classes in that level. [Previous Chapter 1](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/blob/03cc4fc1bad78d33c2a40bbfa545dea3ac9aa29f/docs/01-certificates.md) · [Revised Chapter 1](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/blob/01b48136366f28affae56762c42d707642baa0d5/docs/01-certificates.md) · [PR 234](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/pull/234)

The revised overview formulates the universal sextic question using all odd degree-12 monomials at `q=2`. A degree-six numerator `A` at `q=1` represents the same form there because `A Ω / F² = (AF) Ω / F³`. For this pair the book gives equivalence of certificate existence with poles confined to the same support. In the forward direction a certificate field `v` gives `Fv`. In the reverse direction, existence for the specific numerator `AF` at `q=2` implies existence for `A` at `q=1` on that support. Each catalogue entry still carries its own fixed numerator and pole order. ## Solve proposals and the maintained Lean library

fqlx’s merged policy change raises the limit from **64 to 256 files** in one existing instance folder. The count includes the manifest, explanation, submitted Lean modules and any optional witness. The policy allows 16 MiB of source, 60 minutes in total and 14 GiB of memory. Each submitted Lean module goes through compilation and independent replay within that budget. [Machine-readable policy](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/blob/01b48136366f28affae56762c42d707642baa0d5/.github/solve-policy.json) · [Contributor instructions](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/blob/01b48136366f28affae56762c42d707642baa0d5/CONTRIBUTING.md) · [PR 236](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics/pull/236)

Upstream moved the single-use cyclic and polar cubic packages into the accepted `cycle-three` and `mixed-polar` solves. Those two applications, `holdout-moving-branch` and `mixed-biweight` now share conic and chart modules and residue calculations. The formal guide credits hodgepodgeconjecture’s cyclic work and fqlx’s transitive construction. PR 246 removed the shared Fermat module, which upstream reports no accepted solve imported. The `fermat-q2` explanation retains the subset-Euler construction. These are changes to the current sources of existing results.
