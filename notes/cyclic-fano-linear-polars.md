# Degree-six polars from linear vector fields

For the fixed `cyclic-triple-fano` target, every nonzero denominator

$$
E_M=(Mx)\cdot\nabla\Delta,\qquad M\in\mathrm{Mat}_{5\times5}(\mathbb C),
$$

is excluded. This extends the method of the [constant-direction polar exclusion](cyclic-fano-polar-family.md) to a 25-parameter degree-six family. The conclusion uses the complete numerator correction space. It does not solve the instance or exclude other degree-six denominators.

The source remains the frozen upstream instance at `d96911d725077e66a8f10848908be01a643b1714`; neither $F$, $q=1$, nor $A$ changes. All calculations below use exact rational coefficients. The final identities consequently hold over $\mathbb C$.

## Construction and complete corrections

Use the same weighted quotient and density as in the [Piola reduction](cyclic-fano-transfer.md):

$$
x=(X_0,X_1,Y_0,Y_1,Y_2),\quad
G=Z^2+Q(x)Z+H(x),\quad
\Delta=Q^2-4H,\quad
\beta=X_0Y_0^3/3.
$$

The five base coordinates have weight one and $Z$ has weight three. Put $L=(Mx)\cdot\partial$ and $\tau=\mathrm{tr}(M)$. An explicit Euler-normalized E1 lift is

$$
P^0_{x_i}=-4\beta(Mx)_i,\qquad P^0_Z=2\beta L(Q),
\qquad P^0(G)=\beta E_M.
$$

The producer checks this identity in all 25 matrix directions, including its upstairs Piola pullback against the original $F,A$. In coordinates $t=2Z+Q$, the field has base components $-4\beta Mx$ and zero $t$ component. Its E2 residual is therefore

$$
R_M=E_M\,\mathrm{div}(P^0)-P^0(E_M)
=4\bigl[\beta L^2(\Delta)-(L(\beta)+\beta\tau)L(\Delta)\bigr].
$$

The trace term is essential: the direction now varies with $x$.

The normalized E1 syzygies are generated by the weighted Koszul fields. One exact regular-sequence control is reduction modulo five: $G$ becomes $Z^2+\sum_i x_i^6$, and its gradient becomes nonzero multiples of $Z,x_0^5,\ldots,x_4^5$. The same weighted smoothness and Euler-normalization argument used in the previous packet supplies the full correction space over characteristic zero.

At degree six this space contains 85 raw Koszul fields: ten constant base/base fields and 75 base/$Z$ fields with quadratic multipliers. The E2 quotient has weight four, giving 75 multiples of $G$, including the five multipliers $Zx_i$. None is discarded from the checks.

Since $E_M$ is even under $t\mapsto-t$, averaging a compatible numerator over this involution preserves both certificate equations. For a raw constant base/base field

$$
K_{ij}=G_j\partial_i-G_i\partial_j,
$$

the averaged E2 image is

$$
-\frac14 W_{ij}(E_M),\qquad
W_{ij}=\Delta_i\partial_j-\Delta_j\partial_i.
$$

Every base/$Z$ correction has zero averaged image at this degree. Thus ten arbitrary correction constants remain. This is an exact projection of the complete system, not an assumption that those corrections vanish. The producer checks all $25\cdot85=2,125$ raw projection identities and all 75 quotient identities. The averaging operation is the elementary finite-group average discussed in the supplied Shafarevich Appendix §4 excerpt; the explicit identities, rather than the general averaging theorem alone, prove this reduction.

## Elimination with portable identities

The coefficient system is linear in 325 formal monomials $M_{ij}M_{kl}$ and 250 formal products of matrix entries with correction constants. These products are retained as formal columns for extracting necessary equations; every actual solution must satisfy the resulting coefficient identities.

| Stage | Actual map | Exact result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All 25 matrix parameters | 859 active rows, 575 columns | Rank 414; 20 off-diagonal squares are in the rowspace |
| Five diagonal parameters | 219 active rows, 65 columns | Rank 52, including 40 correction pivots; 12 necessary quadrics |
| Linear multiples of the 12 quadrics | 35 cubic monomials, 60 columns | Rank 35; all five diagonal cubes have explicit multipliers |

There are 1,001 possible spatial monomials of degree ten in five variables; 142 rows of the initial map are identically zero. The packet contains every column polynomial, not merely the displayed sparsity pattern.

For each off-diagonal entry, the packet provides a rational coefficient functional whose value on the full physical residual is exactly $M_{ij}^2$ and which annihilates every correction column. Hence all twenty off-diagonal entries vanish over $\mathbb C$.

Write the remaining diagonal as $(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)$. After rebuilding, the twelve quadrics $q_r(a)$ have exact coefficient functionals in the diagonal system. For example, one is $(a_3-a_4)^2$. Degree-two rowspace tests do **not** isolate any individual $a_i^2$; that unsuccessful step is retained. Linear polynomial multipliers then give five identities

$$
a_i^3=\sum_{r=1}^{12}\ell_{ir}(a)q_r(a),\qquad i=0,\ldots,4.
$$

The supplied checker multiplies and sums each identity using `Fraction`. Thus every diagonal entry also vanishes. A complete certificate in this family would require $M=0$.

There is no hidden admissibility exception: the 25 coefficient rows $x_i^5x_j$ give an invertible minor of the map $M\mapsto E_M$. Modulo five this minor is the identity; its nonzero rational determinant is also exported. Every nonzero $M$ therefore yields a nonzero base polynomial. Since $G$ is monic in $Z$, that polynomial is not identically zero on $G=0$. The only remaining matrix gives the forbidden zero denominator.

## Attempts, checks, and limits

The initial modular screen at prime 1009 suggested the twenty square consequences. Exact rational reconstruction then proved them; the modular observation is not the final exclusion argument. A separate SymPy discovery check suggested cubic multipliers for the diagonal stage. The stored proof and its replay require neither SymPy nor a Gröbner-basis solver.

The earlier [probe output](../results/cyclic-fano-linear-polars/probe.json) is retained as an intermediate diagnostic without source bindings. Its square duals and diagonal equations are superseded for verification by the source-bound final packet; the final checker does not trust that probe file.

The first implementation reused a projection helper limited to $Z$ degree two. It correctly stopped on the five quotient columns $GZx_i$, which contain $Z^3$. The helper was replaced by the exact recurrence

$$
e_0=1,\quad e_1=-Q/2,\quad e_n=-Qe_{n-1}-He_{n-2},
$$

for the averaged monic remainder of $Z^n$. The final computation includes all 75 quotient columns. This was an implementation failure caught before any mathematical conclusion was reported as checked.

The final producer and replay took approximately 1.05 and 1.19 seconds locally, each inside a 120-second subprocess limit. No timeout occurred. Nine mutation controls reject deleted basis directions, omitted corrections or quotients, changed dual equations, changed cubic multipliers, and a falsified injectivity determinant.

Independent replay passed all 25 E1/Piola lifts, 1,875 Euler-normalization identities, 325 residual coefficients, 2,125 raw projection identities, 75 quotient directions, 20 square duals, 12 diagonal consequences, and five cube identities. It also recomputed ranks 414, 52, and 35 and the injectivity minor, with eleven altered-input controls rejected.

There is also a theorem-dependent corollary: for each fixed nonzero $M$, every positive power of its denominator is excluded. This applies the repository's $q=1$ prescribed-support theorem under the hypotheses checked in the [support audit](cyclic-fano-support-audit.md). It is not a fresh direct computation for those powers, and the geometric theorem is not formalized by the arithmetic checker.

This is a denominator-family obstruction, not a constructed support, a Lean `HasCertificate` proof, or an accepted solve. It does not exclude arbitrary degree-six polynomials or unions with other supports. Separate failures on two supports do not imply a failure on their union.
