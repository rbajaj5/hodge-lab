## Why the coefficient embedding exists

The old four parameters U,r,w,t form a transcendence basis of L over Q.
Let C be the intermediate field generated by U,a_c,v_c,t. The literal monic
cover equation makes r algebraic over C, and

\[
w=(v_c r-1)/r^2
\]

makes w algebraic over C. Here r is nonzero by the existing independent
parameter theorem; division has a proved denominator condition. The other
two original parameters already lie in C. Transitivity therefore makes
L algebraic over C. The transcendence-degree bound from the original four
independent parameters forces the four new generators U,a_c,v_c,t to be
algebraically independent. Restriction gives independence of a_c,v_c,t.
This is an algebraic/transcendence-degree argument, not a specialization
test or an assumed Jacobian criterion.

On the generic side the proof establishes independence of the actual
named a,v,t in Q(v)(t)(a), using the nested rational-function construction.
It also proves that those three coordinates generate the whole existing
field: a subfield containing rational coefficients and each successive
rational-function variable contains every rational function. The two
coordinate fraction-field descriptions then construct \(\iota\).
`genericCoordinateEquiv` identifies the existing K, rather than substituting
an unrelated rational-function field and assuming its compatibility.

The module names the rational-algebra instances on each nested field
explicitly. External callers can install the exported
`vRationalAlgebra`, `coeffRationalAlgebra`, and `baseRationalAlgebra`
instances, as the positive controls do. This prevents elaboration from
choosing different definitionally presented instances. Both
`baseToCurve_rational` and `curve_scalar_tower` prove the actual Q-scalar

## Trace statements, with the correct algebra

For every e in E and every z in \(T=L\otimes_K E\), Lean proves

\[
\iota(\operatorname{Tr}_{E/K}(e))
=\operatorname{Tr}_{T/L}(1\otimes e)
=\sum_{j=0}^{17}\sigma_j(e),
\]
\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{T/L}(z)
=\operatorname{Tr}_{L^{18}/L}(\beta(z))
=\sum_{j=0}^{17}\beta(z)_j.
\]

Base change uses Mathlib's multiplication-operator and linear-map trace
base-change theorems. Transport uses the constructed algebra isomorphism.
The all-sheet sum also agrees with the earlier splitting-field trace
theorem through the proved embedding. For arbitrary \(\ell\in L\),

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{T/L}(\ell\otimes e)
=\ell\,\iota(\operatorname{Tr}_{E/K}(e)).
\]

In particular the source scalar has all-sheet trace zero, although its
physical single-sheet value above is nonzero. The controls distinguish

\(2\otimes1\) (equal to 36). No field trace of the algebraic closure L
over K is substituted for the degree-18 algebra trace, and no
\([L:K]\) multiplier is introduced.
