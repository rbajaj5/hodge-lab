## The nine choices and their exact outcomes

Write the weighted quotient as $G=Z^2+QZ+H$, with $Z=X_2^3$ and weights
$(1,1,3,1,1,1)$. Let $T=-X_1-iY_2$, $L=Y_0-iX_1$, and use the exact
coefficients $\mu_3,\gamma$ in the higher unfolding packet to define

$$
C=X_1^2T-\mu_3L^3,\qquad S=C^2+\gamma L^6,
\qquad R=X_1^2+Y_2^2.
$$

The expressions $C,S$ extend checked local jets away from their target
surface by a chosen homogenization. They are not known global support
equations. $R$ is the rational conjugate product of $T$.

## Why the Jacobian repair is a complete test

The preceding [character/Piola transfer](cyclic-fano-transfer.md) reduces
an invariant denominator $D=E(X_0,X_1,X_2^3,Y_0,Y_1,Y_2)$ to

$$
P(G)=\beta E,\qquad
B_E(P)=E\,\mathrm{div}P-P(E)\equiv0\pmod G,
\qquad \beta=X_0Y_0^3/3.
$$

For weighted degree $d$, coordinate $P_i$ has weight $d+w_i-2$.
Character projection retains a solution whenever one exists, so the
restriction to this numerator character does not lose solutions for the
fixed invariant denominator. All numerator coefficients are included for
the first six tests. The second equation permits a quotient of weight
$2d-8$, including its full nonzero space whenever that weight is nonnegative.

For $E=G_ZK$, set $P^{(0)}_Z=\beta K$ and all other coordinates to zero.
This satisfies E1 exactly. Every other normalized solution is

$$
P=P^{(0)}+\sum_{i<j}H_{ij}(G_j e_i-G_i e_j),
\qquad \deg H_{ij}=d+w_i+w_j-8.
$$

Completeness uses the regular sequence of weighted partial derivatives of
$G$. The original $F$ is smooth: reduction modulo 5 is a smooth Fermat
sextic. A nonzero common zero of the partials of $G$ would lift under the
finite surjective map $Z=X_2^3$ to a nonzero common zero of the partials
of $F$, a contradiction. Thus the six partials of $G$ have height six
in the six-variable polynomial ring and form a regular sequence. Their
first syzygies are generated by the displayed Koszul relations. Taking
the required homogeneous component gives every allowed correction.

The repaired tests solve

$$
\sum_{i<j} B_E\bigl(H_{ij}(G_j e_i-G_i e_j)\bigr)-GQ_0
=-B_E(P^{(0)})=2\beta K^2,\qquad \deg Q_0=2d-8.
$$

The last equality uses that $\beta,K$ are independent of $Z$ and
$G_{ZZ}=2$. It exposes the exact residual that the corrections must cancel.

The short E2 obstructions therefore reject the repaired denominators with
the entire correction space, including exceptional coefficient choices.
No rank assumption or vanishing quotient is imposed. All nine denominators
are nonzero modulo $F$: below degree six this is automatic; at degree six
their $Z$ degree is at most one, whereas $G$ has a nonzero $Z^2$ term.

## Reproduction, bounded attempts, and limits

The [packet directory](../results/cyclic-fano-guided-denominators/) contains
all nine denominators, column labels, coefficient rows, exact duals, and
120-second bounded worker logs. Arithmetic uses rational real and imaginary
parts; no numerical tolerance or finite-field inference is used. All workers
completed in under two seconds on this machine, including process overhead.
No candidate produced a certificate.

```sh
python -S experiments/cyclic_fano_guided_denominators.py list
python -S experiments/cyclic_fano_guided_denominators.py run --candidate jacobian_center_cubic
python -S experiments/cyclic_fano_guided_denominators.py verify
python -S experiments/check_cyclic_fano_guided_denominators.py
python experiments/plot_cyclic_fano_higher_unfolding.py
```

Use `run --candidate ID` for each of the nine IDs printed by `list` to repeat
discovery; `verify` replays the saved exact obstructions and rejects a changed
dual for each candidate. The independent checker rebuilds the physical
operators from the protected source with separate polynomial arithmetic.
Receipts bind source, producer, arithmetic dependencies, and packet hashes.
Primary replay passes all nine tests and nine altered-dual controls. The
independent replay passes all 3,129 physical columns, the weighted Euler
normalization and Piola transfers, and eighteen mutation controls. These
are exact algebraic checks over $\mathbb Q(i)$; the resulting linear
obstructions remain valid after extending scalars to $\mathbb C$.

The useful next constraint is now clearer: a candidate suggested by branch
geometry must survive E1, and multiplying by $G_Z$ alone does not suffice
for these three factors. The finite local jets do not determine a global
support equation. Higher powers, other extensions off the chosen surface,
and other denominators remain open. No Lean `HasCertificate` proof or
accepted solve is claimed.
