INDEPENDENT REASONING REVIEW (NO NEW SYMBOLIC RUN FOR THESE CLAIMS)
1. With chi_t=0 and the packet column convention,
 J_v=chi_v e1/(36t^2)-chi M_a e0/(36t), J_t=chi K,
has exactly
 Div_N J-R0=chi[(partial_t+N_t)K-T],
 T=(partial_b M_a+M_b M_a)e0/36.
The chi_v coefficient cancels because M_b e1=M_a e0; differentiating the
physical coefficient M_a in v gives t partial_b M_a. This retains the
moving-root derivatives of chi_v and chi_vv and both connection rows.
There is no a-flux whose derivative could have been omitted.

2. This factorization applies to the ACTUAL physical chi. It does not need
chi,chi_v,chi_vv to be algebraically independent formal jets. The previously
established physical chi is nonzero (its reused value at r=1,v=2 is -5/3),
so equality of this current with R0 requires the base equation
 (partial_t+N_t)K=T.
The exact displayed family is therefore excluded on the actual cover as
well as in its formal derivation, provided K remains in the specified base
field. Allowing different a/v fluxes or more general cover-dependent
coefficient functions is outside this reviewed family.

3. For generic a,v, N_t and T have no finite t poles outside Delta. The
literal M_a first column and M_b entries show T has at most Delta^-2.
A rational K cannot have a pole at an ordinary finite point because its
highest derivative term would have nothing to cancel it.
At a simple discriminant point, the saved generic trace/determinant
identities give residue eigenvalues 0,-1/3. If K had order m>1, its leading
coefficient would be multiplied by R-m I at order m+1>2, where the source
has no term. This matrix is invertible; hence only simple Delta poles are
possible. Generic squarefreeness follows from the preserved-degree,
squarefree specialization a=3,v=2, with leading coefficient 27a^2!=0.

4. The infinity gauge has the correct sign:
 H=G K, G=diag(t^-3,t^-1),
 H'+[G N_t G^-1-G'G^-1]H=G T.
The structure code computes generic entry orders (-3,-2,-2,-3) for that
gauged connection and (-6,-6) for its source. If H had polynomial growth
m>=1, its derivative's degree m-1 could not cancel against a connection
term of degree at most m-2 or the decaying source. Therefore H=O(1), so
K0=O(t^3), K1=O(t). With at most simple Delta poles and deg Delta=12,
K=P/Delta has the claimed bounds deg P0<=15, deg P1<=13. A monic
normalization of Delta changes only nonzero base-field factors.

5. Cramer's rule is valid here. The bounded coefficients of P give a fixed
finite system over Q(a,v), regular at (3,2); monic normalization only
requires a!=0 there. Full column rank 30 at that point gives a 30x30
minor invertible in the local ring. A hypothetical generic solution must
equal the inverse-minor solution and hence be regular there. It would
specialize to a solution of the remaining equations, contradicting the
verified exact left-null inconsistency certificate. Thus this is not an
unsupported inference from failure at one specialization. Extra generic
equations would only strengthen the contradiction; the checked submatrix
already supplies the required minor and incompatible equation.

R0=Div_N Y+R2. Consequently

This is a mixed v,t-current exclusion, not a new pure-t source exclusion.
