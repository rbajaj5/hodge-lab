1. Precise all-sheet physical identity

Let K=Q(a,v,t), f(R)=R^18+R^12+(vR-1)^6-aR^15, and let rho_j
be all 18 roots on the generic etale open. Put V=r U in the established
physical field, retain its z, and put
 P(V)=aV^3+tvV^2+t(1-t^2)V/7+1+t^6, z^6=-P(V).
For each j there is a Q-algebra map from the ALLOWED regular domain
 S0=Q[U,r,w,t,z,z^-1]
to the common physical algebraic closure, given by
 U |-> V/rho_j, r |-> rho_j, w |-> (v rho_j-1)/rho_j^2,
 t |-> t, z |-> z.
The roots are nonzero because f(0)=1. These maps kill the curve relation,
fix V=rU after pullback, and fix z. They induce universal differential and
exterior maps. No map from the entire old ambient fraction field killing
G is used, and no inverse of G is introduced. If the original square-cover
chart is needed, choose x_j^2=V/rho_j in the algebraic closure; its sign has
no effect on this reflection-quotient normalized source.

The exact generic-sheet calculation is
 p(V/R,R,(vR-1)/R^2,t)-P(V) = V^3 f(R)/R^15,
 d(V/R) wedge dR wedge d((vR-1)/R^2) wedge dt
   = R^13/f_R(R) dV wedge da wedge dv wedge dt.
The differentiated sheet equation supplies R_a=R^15/f_R and
R_v=-6R(vR-1)^5/f_R. Thus the actual regular normalized source maps to
 S_j = (5/72) [rho_j^11/f'(rho_j)] V^2/z^11
           dV wedge da wedge dv wedge dt.

Consequently the PREVIOUSLY PROVED scalar sum sum_j rho_j^11/f'(rho_j)=0
implies the literal full four-form equality sum_j S_j=0. This is not a new
scalar trace replay: the new ingredient is common physical sheet maps fixing
V,z and the full differential Jacobian.

Write phi_j=rho_j^11/f'(rho_j), B=da wedge dv wedge dt and use the literal
two-row b-column (M_b e0)=(M0,M1)^T and its exact correction R_b0(V).
Then the inherited source-reduction identity gives, on every sheet,
 S_j = (phi_j/12) (M0+M1 V) dV/z^5 wedge B
         + d(phi_j R_b0(V)/(12 z^5)) wedge B.
Both compact rows, the exact correction, and the unreduced source separately
sum to zero, because M0,M1,V,z,R_b0 and B are common to all sheets. The
correction sums to d[(sum_j phi_j)R_b0/(12z^5)] wedge B=0. No row is dropped.
For this reduction also require Delta!=0; the bare source map needs no Delta.
All signs here use the fibre-first normalized-source orientation.

2. Why this cancellation does not solve the source equation

The trace map discards exactly the summand in which this source lives. In
the finite-cover de Rham complex, P_tf=Id-(1/18) unit*Tr commutes with the
connection. Since Tr(source)=0, P_tf(source)=source, not zero. A primitive
can be projected to the trace-free summand if one already has a primitive.
There remain 17 cover coefficients times both compact rows, hence the same
rank-34 equation. The source lies in power slot 11, but derivatives mix
cover slots; none of the other 16 trace-free slots can be deleted on that
ground. All-sheet cancellation supplies no physical single-sheet current.

4. What the new current enables, and what it does not

The published residual had a naked t^-2 pole introduced by integration by
parts. The new Q and R2 permit a subsequent current search with no required
t=0 pole; the inhomogeneous numerator there is explicitly the vector above.
The short Q uses only the cover scalars psi and psi_v. This is a candidate
simplification of the inhomogeneous equation, not a lower-rank theorem or
a proof that a restricted denominator ansatz is complete. It restores both
compact rows in the residual, can introduce a^-1, and retains ramification
and discriminant denominators. The original source was already regular at
t=0, so removing this artificial pole is not itself evidence of exactness.
