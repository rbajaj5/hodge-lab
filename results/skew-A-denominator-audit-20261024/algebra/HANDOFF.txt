A-denominator structural reduction, 24 October 2026
===================================================

This is a necessary, source-bound reduction of the proposed six-current
family. It constructs neither a primitive nor a nonzero kernel. It does not
run a sample matrix search. The original 702 numerator coefficients reduce
to 294 possible solution coefficients before imposing the remaining full
two-row operator equation. The same reduction applies to every homogeneous
kernel of that original finite family.

1. The new and old scalar families have zero intersection
--------------------------------------------------------
If L lies in B and L=P/A with P in B, then A L=P. Over Q(r,t)[v],
deg_v(A L)=6+deg_v(L)>2 for every nonzero L. Thus L=P=0.
Consequently B and B/A have no overlap, including after constant-field
extension. A proposed sum of all six old and new sectors starts with 1404
independent coefficients; it is not an inclusion of the old box in the new.

2. The A^-2 coefficient forces normal tangency
---------------------------------------------
Directly from the pinned matrices,
  R_a=Res_{a=0}(M_a da)=[[0,-c/(6b)],[0,-1/3]],
  det(R_a-I)=4/3.
M_b,M_c,M_d and both rows of S have no a-pole at the generic a=0
boundary away from b Delta=0. These statements are symbolically checked
from the literal JSON, not taken only from its descriptive residue field.

The divisor A=0 is prime and smooth in Spec R. Indeed the invertible
Laurent coordinate change w=(rv-1)/r^2 gives
  r^3 A=w^6+r^6+1.
The polynomial w^6+r^6+1 is Eisenstein at the prime r^2+1 of Q[r]: its
constant coefficient is divisible once by r^2+1; all intermediate
coefficients are zero; its leading coefficient is1. Gauss's lemma gives
irreducibility, preserved by adjoining t and localizing at r. Its derivative
in r is6r^5, a unit on r!=0, so its divisor is smooth in characteristic zero.
The generic divisor is away from b=0 and Delta=0: H=r^15 A has v-degree6,
whereas b has degree1 and Delta|a=0,b=tv has nonzero degree3. Thus neither
can be divisible by H. All boundary arguments below are at this generic
divisor, NOT at an ordinary point in the allowed open r*A*Delta!=0.

Put Q=A_r P_r+A_v P_v (a two-vector). The only order-two A-pole in
Div_N(P/A) is

The -I comes from differentiating 1/A. The R_a comes from M_a dA.
Other terms have at most simple A-poles. Because S has no A-pole and
R_a-I is invertible over the divisor function field, Q=0 modulo A.
The prime ideal (A) in R is the intersection with that divisor's generic
valuation ideal. Thus this is divisibility in R, not merely pointwise
vanishing on a chosen specialization. It applies independently to both
compact rows. No field extension or sheet trace is used.

3. The full low-v-degree tangent module has one generator
--------------------------------------------------------
Set x=rv-1, C=r^18+r^12, H=x^6+C, and
  B0=6r^11(2r^6+1), lambda=(2r^6+1)/(r^6+1).
Over Q(r,t), write
  P_r=p0+p1*x+p2*x^2,
  P_v=q0+q1*x+q2*x^2,
  E=P_r+r^2 P_v.
The normal tangency condition is equivalently H | H_r P_r+H_v P_v.
Its exact remainder is
  B0 P_r + (6/r)[ E0*x^5-C*E1-C*E2*x ].
The coefficients of x^5,x^2,x^0,x^1 force respectively
  E0=0, p2=0, E1=lambda*p0, E2=lambda*p1.
There are exactly four independent equations (an explicit nonzero
four-square minor is saved), so this is the complete solution, not merely
a sufficient syzygy. Consequently
  (P_r,P_v)=(T_r,T_v) h,
  T_r=r^2(r^6+1),
  T_v=r(2r^6+1)v-(3r^6+2),
with deg_v h<=1 initially over Q(r,t).

There is no hidden denominator in h. The ring R is a UFD; the two
polynomials T_r,T_v are coprime there. For example the possible nonunit
content factor r^6+1 of T_r is coprime to the constant-v coefficient
3r^6+2 of T_v. Any denominator of h in lowest terms would divide both
T_r and T_v, which is impossible. Hence h belongs to R.

4. Support endpoints are exact; cancellations cannot enlarge the box
-------------------------------------------------------------------
View h as a Laurent polynomial in r with coefficients in Q[v,t].
The leading r term of T_r is r^8, so max_r(T_r h)=max_r(h)+8;
its extreme coefficient cannot cancel with lower r terms. Therefore
max_r(h)<=-6. The unique trailing r term of T_v is the nonzero constant
-2, so min_r(T_v h)=min_r(h), forcing min_r(h)>=-10.
The leading v coefficient of T_v is r(2r^6+1), nonzero in Q[r,r^-1,t],
so deg_v(T_v h)=deg_v(h)+1 and deg_v(h)<=1. The factors T_r,T_v are
independent of t, so the t support is exactly that of h, namely0..2.
Conversely, every h in the box m=-10..-6,n=0..1,k=0..2 produces P_r,P_v
inside the original box. The sparse injection checks all these supports.
There are5*2*3=30 coefficients per compact row, hence60 for h, plus234
unrestricted P_t coefficients. This gives294 possible solution parameters.

6. Rank and kernel limits
-------------------------
Every exact homogeneous kernel in the new family lies in im(E), hence has
dimension at most294. This is a rank LOWER bound408 for the full operator,
not the characteristic-zero rank UPPER bound needed to certify a deficient
modular inconsistency. No nonzero exact kernel is supplied here.

If a reduced modular matrix has full294 coefficient-column rank and an
augmented295-square minor is nonzero, the necessary family is excluded
over Q. If its modular rank drops, modular augmented inconsistency alone
does not exclude Q solutions. One still needs either enough exact rational
kernel vectors (independent over Q) to give a matching characteristic-zero
rank upper bound, or an exact left-null witness/nonzero pairing or other
exact inconsistency certificate. The injection is not such a witness.
