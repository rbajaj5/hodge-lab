Family and full operator
------------------------
Let B=span_Q{r^m v^n t^k:-10<=m<=2,0<=n,k<=2}. Write the six scalar
components of the physical current as
  F=P0+P1/A+P2/A^2,
where each vector component of every Pi belongs to B. Use
  A=r^3+r^-3+(rv-1)^6/r^15,
  b=tv, c=t(1-t^2)/7, d=1+t^6,
  N=(A_r M_a,A_v M_a+t M_b,N_t),
  N_t=v M_b+c' M_c+d' M_d,
  S=r^-4 M_b e0/12.
All six components, both rows and all r,v,t derivatives are retained.

The three scalar sectors are linearly independent: multiplying a proposed
relation by A^2 gives A^2P0+AP1+P2=0. Their possible nonzero v-degree bands
are12..14,6..8,0..2, so highest-degree comparison successively forces all

Full reduced top operator and exact next-pole condition
-------------------------------------------------------
Put ell=3r(r^6-1) and tau2=div(T)-2ell=r(4r^6+9). Then
  Div_N(P2/A^2)=W2/A^2,
  W2=[T_r partial_r+T_v partial_v+tau2 I
          +ell*A*M_a+t*T_v*M_b]h +(partial_t+N_t)k.
W2 is regular at the generic A divisor. With D_N denoting the complete
physical divergence on an unquotiented current, the full equation is

  A^2 D_N P0 + A D_N P1 - Q1 + W2 = A^2 S.

Restricting this equation to A=0 yields the coupled condition
  (R-I) Q1 + W2|A=0 = 0.
Writing W=(W0,W1)=W2|A=0, it is equivalent to
  Q1_1=(3/4)W1,
  Q1_0=W0-c W1/(8b).
The off-diagonal term matters. These are normal-flux targets, not completed
polynomial lifts for P1. A nonzero W can be cancelled by a non-tangent P1.

New second-row compatibility projection
----------------------------------------
The preceding matrices give exactly
  W1=[T_r partial_r+T_v partial_v
          +2r^7+19r/2+(3r^6+2)/(2v)]h1
          +(partial_t-1/(2t))k1.
Write h1=H0(r,t)+v H1(r,t), and let k1 have v-degree at most2. Every
term above has polynomial v-degree at most2 except
  (3r^6+2)H0/(2v).

Use x=rv-1, C=r^18+r^12, H=x^6+C. The imported normal-flux remainder
of any P1r,P1v with v-degree<=2 has support only x^0,x^1,x^2,x^5:
  H_r P1r+H_v P1v mod H
   =B0 P1r+(6/r)[E0*x^5-C*E1-C*E2*x],
  B0=6r^11(2r^6+1), E=P1r+r^2 P1v.
The extra r^-15 factor giving Q1 does not change that support.

In Q(r,t)[x]/(H), the exact inverse is
  1/v = -r*(x^5-x^4+x^3-x^2+x-1)/(C+1).
Consequently the forbidden x^3 and x^4 coefficients of W1 are
  (-mu H0, +mu H0), mu=r(3r^6+2)/(2(C+1)).
Here mu is a nonzero characteristic-zero rational function. Since Q1_1
must equal3W1/4 and Q1_1 has neither power, H0=0 identically.
