Pinned repositories under `sources/` are Git submodules, not relicensed copies. Their authorship, notices, and any licenses remain with those sources. Public availability alone does not imply an open-source license; consult each source before redistribution or reuse.

The orchestration, checking code, experiment adaptations, figures, and notes in this repository were produced for this research notebook with AI assistance. `SOURCES.md` identifies direct code reuse, adaptations, conceptual inspiration, and references that were only inspected. No claim of novelty or correctness is inferred from a source repository's title. The exact scope of each executed check is recorded with its output. Private correspondence is not included.
